# cr v0.10.0 — The next step

A round is about a dozen commands, and only three of them need judgement:
extracting the claims, reviewing the units, and reading the draft. Until now the
rest of the sequence lived in the operator's head. v0.10 adds `cr next`, which
reads it out of state. The contract is [`spec/0.10.0.md`](0.10.0.md); only
§10.4 and its row in §11 are new.

## `cr next <pr>`

Prints every step the round still owes, in order, each with who takes it — cr,
the agent, or the human — why it is owed, the exact commands, and the items it
is owed for:

1. `brief`: no round has been opened, or the head moved. It is then the only step.
2. `claims` and `intent`: the intent pass has not recorded its claims or its
   mapping.
3. `record`: a role wrote a `review-*.ndjson` or `proposals-*.ndjson` in the
   round's fan-out holding ids the round does not hold, unless the round's last
   merge or record ran after the file was written.
4. `review`: cells are missing, named by `unit/role`.
5. `settle`: claims mapped to no unit.
6. `draft`: records in draft or queued. This is the human's step, and it never
   prints `--confirm`.
7. `recheck`: posted records awaiting a verdict.

It writes nothing, reads no revision, and forms no opinion: every step is a fact
about files cr already keeps. A round that owes nothing reports no step.

## Measured

Its first run was on two real rounds kept by hand. On tarfin-labs/backend#6298
it named the one step left, the human's read of six queued questions. On
tarfin-labs/backend#6292 it found three `proposals-test-adequacy.ndjson` files
that were written during the round and never recorded, which nobody had noticed.

## What it does not do

It prints the steps whose actor is cr and does not run them. Whether it should
is a decision, and ROADMAP records it. Cells are not found in the fan-out,
because no prompt names a cells path, so a missing cell is reported as missing
whether or not a role wrote it somewhere.
